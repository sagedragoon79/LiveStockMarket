# Change notes (Steam Workshop format)

Newest first. Paste the BBCode block of a version into the Workshop change note when publishing it.

## 1.0.0 — September 5, 2026

```
[h2]1.0.0 — Initial release[/h2]
[list]
[*]Goats / Sheep switch on every goat barn (both tiers), saved with the barn. A Sheep Barn's animals become sheep, newborns and purchased goats arrive as sheep, and the herders shear wool in season.
[*]Wool: a new item, stored with hides, counted in the Settlement Items window, carried by the agricultural and hunter-and-herder traders, traded at the Trading Center.
[*]Shearing: fleece grows over 240 days, shorn once a year during days 78 to 200, 4 wool per fully grown sheep. Growth banks across a switch to Goats and back.
[*]Wool garments: Winter Boots (Cobbler Shop), Winter Cloak (Tannery) and Woolen Clothes (Weaver) as extra recipes beside the vanilla ones. Each is a trade good and a 25% warmer replacement for shoes, hide coat and linen clothes; villagers prefer them while in stock.
[*]Sheep model, name and icon on the animals; sheep icons in the barn window; "Shearing Sheep" herder task; worn garments show their own icon in the villager window.
[*]Settings in Keep Clarity's F10 panel (or MelonPreferences.cfg): garments, shearing and visuals, nearly all live.
[/list]
```
